## The story only you could tell

Find an original opening that leaves readers wanting more.

**Compatible tools:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Tags:** Creative writing, Fiction, Storytelling

```text

Propose three distinct openings, each under 200 words, with a vivid setting, a character desire, and a subtle point of tension. Avoid clichés and imitating a living author. Then explain what narrative promise each opening makes and suggest the next scene.
```
