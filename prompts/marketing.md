## Find your brand’s real voice

**Compatible tools:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Tags:** Branding, Voice, Strategy

Include three voice traits, what each means and does not mean, vocabulary guidance, and do/don’t examples for a website headline, email, and social post.
